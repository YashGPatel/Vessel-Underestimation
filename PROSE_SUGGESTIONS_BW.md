# BW Trends to Report in Results (observations only, no interpretation)

## Trend 1: Slopes decreased with threshold for BOTH MIP and MeIP, across ALL contrast conditions
- MIP pre: 0.61 → 0.24 | MeIP pre: 0.83 → 0.53
- Same pattern at peak and post

## Trend 2: MeIP slopes were consistently higher than MIP at every threshold and contrast condition
- e.g. BW-1 pre: MeIP 0.83 vs MIP 0.61
- e.g. BW-5 pre: MeIP 0.53 vs MIP 0.24

## Trend 3: MIP slopes dropped more steeply than MeIP
- MIP pre drops by 0.37 (0.61 → 0.24)
- MeIP pre drops by 0.30 (0.83 → 0.53)

## Trend 4: R² behaviour was opposite for MIP vs MeIP
- MIP R² degraded sharply with threshold (pre: 0.58 → 0.24, post: 0.76 → 0.34)
- MeIP R² remained stable or slightly improved (pre: 0.59 → 0.65, peak: 0.78 → 0.89)

## Trend 5: MeIP at low thresholds overestimated at peak and post-contrast
- BW-1 peak: m = 1.14, BW-1 post: m = 1.11
- Sweet spot around BW-3 (peak m = 0.99, post m = 0.97)
- MIP never overestimated (highest was BW-1 peak = 0.96)

## Trend 6: Best BW still had lower R² than model-based
- Best BW: MeIP BW-3 peak, m = 0.99, R² = 0.86
- Best model: GG 1/e² MeIP post, m = 1.00, R² = 0.97
