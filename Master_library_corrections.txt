CORRECTIONS MADE TO Merkle_Master_Library.bib
Vessel Underestimation manuscript, reference list clean-up
Date: 17 September 2026

These entries were edited locally while fixing the reference list of the
Vessel Underestimation manuscript. They are corrections to the source data,
not to the citation style, so they should also be applied in Zotero -- 
otherwise the next Better BibTeX export will undo them.

A backup of the file before these edits is at Merkle_Master_Library.bib.bak.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. huangOpticalCoherenceTomography1991
   field:  journal
   was:    Science (New York, N.Y.)
   now:    Science
   why:    The jabbrv package (loaded by optica-article.cls) abbreviates
           journal names at typesetting time, but leaves one-word titles
           alone. With the PubMed-style parenthetical the title counted as
           several words, so it printed as "Sci. (New York, N.Y.)".

2. kornfieldRegulationBloodFlow2014
   field:  journal
   was:    The Journal of Neuroscience
   now:    Journal of Neuroscience
   why:    Printed as "The J. Neurosci.".

3. kornfieldRegulationBloodFlow2014
   field:  pages
   was:    11504 LP  - 11513
   now:    11504--11513
   why:    "LP" is a HighWire page-marker artifact from the publisher's
           citation export. Printed as "11504 LP - 11513".

4. merkleDynamicContrastOptical2016
   field:  pages
   was:    4289
   now:    4289--4312
   why:    Only the first page was stored. Correct range per the article
           record (Biomed. Opt. Express 7(10), 4289-4312).

5. augustinMultifunctionalOCTImage2016
   field:  pages
   was:    OTh4C--2
   now:    OTh4C.2
   why:    This is a conference paper number, not a page range. The double
           hyphen made BibTeX print it as "OTh4C-2".

6. augustinMultifunctionalOCTImage2016
   field:  organization  (removed)
   was:    organization = {Optica Publishing Group}
   why:    The entry also has publisher = {Optical Society of America}, so
           the reference printed two publishers:
           "Optica Publishing Group (Optical Society of America, 2016)".

7. augustinOpticalCoherenceTomography2020
   field:  title
   was:    Optical {{Coherence Tomography Findings}} in the {{Retinas}} of
           {{SOD1 Knockout Mice}}
   now:    Optical Coherence Tomography Findings in the Retinas of {SOD1}
           Knockout Mice
   why:    The double braces forced Title Case, making this the only
           title-cased reference in an otherwise sentence-case list. Only
           SOD1 needs protecting.

8. sharifizadFactorsDeterminingFlickerinduced2016
   field:  journal
   was:    Investigative Ophthalmology and Visual Science
   now:    Investigative Ophthalmology \& Visual Science
   why:    Printed as "Investig. Ophthalmol. Vis. Sci." while every other
           IOVS reference printed "Investig. Ophthalmol. & Vis. Sci.".
   note:   The master library holds 17 entries spelled with "&", 20 with
           "and" and 1 all-lowercase. Only the entry cited here was
           changed; the rest are still inconsistent.

9. rabioloComparisonMethodsQuantify2018
   field:  journal
   was:    PloS One
   now:    PLOS ONE
   why:    Printed as "PloS One" next to "PLoS ONE" from another entry.

10. augustinMultifunctionalOCTEnables2016
   field:  journal
   was:    PLoS ONE
   now:    PLOS ONE
   why:    Same inconsistency as above.
   note:   The master library holds four spellings of this journal
           (PLOS ONE, PLoS ONE, PloS One, PloS one). Only the two entries
           cited here were changed.
